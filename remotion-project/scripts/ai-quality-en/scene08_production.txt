So, what role does AI play in the mass production stage?

AI monitors particle size distribution in real-time. 24 hours a day, without rest.

When particle size data comes from sensors, AI compares it against the target range.

If the D50 value exceeds 18 micrometers, AI issues an adjustment command to increase rotation speed.

Conversely, if D50 is below 12 micrometers, it commands to decrease rotation speed.

If distribution deviation suddenly increases, it sends a warning alert to inspect blade condition.

This is how AI performs real-time feedback control.

Here's the key point.

AI only manages physical size. Chemical recovery rate is still managed by humans through periodic ICP sampling.

However, if particle size is maintained consistently, recovery rate also remains stable, as this correlation has already been verified through human experimentation.

AI's particle size management therefore directly contributes to quality stabilization.
