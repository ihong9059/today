So how do we determine quality?

The most important indicator is the recovery rate. It represents the ratio of valuable metals actually recovered compared to those contained in the input waste batteries.

Component purity is also important. It indicates how much lithium, cobalt, and nickel are contained in the black mass.

Now, here's an important point.

This type of chemical component analysis can only be accurately measured through an ICP analyzer, that is, Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission Spectrometry.

Collecting black mass samples, analyzing them with ICP equipment, and verifying the results must be performed by humans.
