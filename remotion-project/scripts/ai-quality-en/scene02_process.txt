First, let's briefly look at the entire process.

The battery recycling process consists of three main stages.

First, primary crushing. Using a twin-shaft shear crusher, waste batteries are broken into pieces of 50 to 100 millimeters.

Second, secondary crushing. Through a hammer crusher, pieces are further reduced to 5 to 20 millimeters.

Third, the separation process. Using an impact mill, valuable metals are separated from the piece surfaces.

After this process, we finally obtain black mass, a powder containing valuable metals such as lithium, cobalt, and nickel.

The two key goals of the process are high recovery rate and uniform quality black mass production.
