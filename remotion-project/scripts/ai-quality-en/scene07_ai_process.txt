Let me organize the AI learning process in three stages.

Stage 1, Goal Setting. This is decided by humans.

Through multiple repeated experiments, we confirm optimal recovery rate conditions, record the particle size distribution characteristics under those conditions, and tell the AI this particle size distribution is the target.

For example, D10 is 5 micrometers, D50 is 15 micrometers, D90 is 30 micrometers, standard deviation within 8 micrometers. We define targets like this.

Stage 2, Data Collection.

Input data includes equipment and environmental conditions. Blade angle, rotation speed, feed rate, temperature, humidity, pressure, and so on.

Output data is the measured particle size distribution. D10, D50, D90 values and standard deviation.

While operating the process, we accumulate combination data of inputs and outputs.

Stage 3, Model Training.

AI receives input data and trains a model to predict particle size distribution.

It compares predicted values with actual measurements and improves the model to reduce errors.

When training is complete, AI understands the relationship: with these equipment and environmental conditions, this particle size distribution will result.
